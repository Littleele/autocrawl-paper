  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: Existing reliance measures commonly infer appropriate or inappropriate reliance from decision outcomes, such as whether a response is correct. Method: The researchers collected responses from undergraduate students after two Generative-AI-supported problem-solving activities. An exploratory factor analysis was conducted on 800 responses from the first activity to identify the scale structure.
