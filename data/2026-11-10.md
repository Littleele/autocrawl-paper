TL;DR: A quasi-experimental study reports that an 8-week program of structured ChatGPT-mediated speaking practice produced substantially larger gains in English oral proficiency than human pair work among 380 Iranian learners. Method: The researchers used a quasi-experimental design with 380 learners assigned to a ChatGPT-practice group or a human-pair-work control group. Both groups completed three 30-minute sessions per week for eight weeks. Oral proficiency was measured with a CEFR-aligned Oral Proficiency Interview assessing fluency, pronunciation, lexical resource, grammatical accuracy, and interactive communication. Conclusion: Structured ChatGPT-mediated speaking practice appears to produce meaningful short-term gains in oral proficiency relative to human pair work in this sample.
